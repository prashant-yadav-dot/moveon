About MoveOn

MoveOn is a digital healing and self-growth platform designed to help people take small, meaningful steps after heartbreak, emotional pain, loneliness or difficult relationships.

Our approach

Healing is personal. MoveOn focuses on practical reflection, calming exercises, daily challenges and progress tracking so you can build supportive habits at your own pace.

A private experience

Personal areas such as your journal, mood history, profile and progress are kept behind account access. The public website only explains the platform and its features.
