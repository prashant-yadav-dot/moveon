A FICTIONAL MOVEON STORY

The Unfinished Love Story

Two students. One first love. One choice that changed everything.

Romeo aur Juliet ek hi school mein padhte the. Class 11 mein shuru hui ek simple si dosti dheere-dheere unki pehli mohabbat ban gayi. Lekin kuch love stories mein problem pyaar nahi hota... problem hoti hai ki duniya un dono ko alag nazar se dekhne lagti hai.

Chapter 01 • Class 11

“Kal bhi miloge?” 💜

School ke corridor mein roz ki chhoti-chhoti baatein, shared lunch, homework ke bahane chats aur woh ek message — “Ghar pahunch gaye?” Romeo ko samajh aa gaya tha ki ye sirf friendship nahi rahi. Juliet bhi usse utna hi chahti thi. Dono ne bina bade promises ke ek doosre ko choose kar liya.

Romeo — simple, emotional, village boy
Juliet — ambitious, confident, city-school girl
Chapter 02 • The Distance

“Tum gaon se ho...”

Romeo gaon mein rehta tha. Uske paas bade kapde, expensive bike ya fancy weekends nahi the — bas apna sachcha dil tha. Kuch mahino baad Juliet ke messages kam hone lage. Replies late aaye. Calls chhoti ho gayi. Romeo har baar khud ko samjhata raha: “Shayad woh busy hogi.”

Chapter 03 • The Betrayal

“Usne kisi aur ko choose kar liya.”

Phir Romeo ko pata chala ki Juliet ne usi school ke ek naye ladke ko apna boyfriend bana liya tha. Romeo ko explanation se zyada silence mila. Jis insaan ke saath usne apna future imagine kiya tha, wahi use peeche chhod kar aage badh chuki thi. Romeo ne na drama kiya, na badla liya — bas har raat apne phone ki screen dekhta raha, shayad ek message aa jaye.

🔒

But that wasn't the end...

Teen mahine baad, ek raat Romeo ke phone par Juliet ka message aaya:

“Romeo... mujhe tumse ek sach kehna hai. Jo tumne us din dekha tha, woh poori kahani nahi thi.”

Romeo ne message padha... aur uski ungli reply button par ruk gayi.

Us raat Juliet ne aisa kya bataya tha jo sab kuch badal sakta tha?

Continue the fictional story and discover what happened that night.
