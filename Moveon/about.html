About MoveOn

Heal • Grow • Be You 🌱

💜 Why MoveOn?

MoveOn is a personal space created for people going through heartbreak, difficult relationships, loneliness and emotional ups and downs. The goal is simple: help you take one small step forward every day.

🌱 Our Mission

Healing does not happen overnight. MoveOn encourages small, consistent steps through journaling, mood tracking, calming activities, challenges and self-reflection.
