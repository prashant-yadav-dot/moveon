About Prashant

Founder & Creator of MoveOn

Building a small space with a big purpose: helping people heal, grow and move forward.

Why MoveOn?

MoveOn is created around a simple belief: difficult days do not define your whole story. It aims to offer a gentle space for reflection, emotional support, self-care and personal growth.

My vision

To make helpful, accessible tools that remind people to value themselves, take one step at a time and build a healthier relationship with their own lives.

What we're building

Healing journals, calming exercises, daily challenges, supportive tools and community features designed to encourage positive habits.
