🔒 Privacy

Your privacy matters to us. MoveOn is designed to give you a private and personal space for your healing journey.

👤 Your Account

Your account information is associated with your Firebase authentication account. Your profile information is used to provide your personalized MoveOn experience.

📖 Journal

Journal entries are intended to remain associated with your MoveOn account. Do not enter passwords, financial information, or other highly sensitive information into your journal.

😊 Mood & Progress

Mood check-ins, challenges and progress information may be stored with your account so MoveOn can show your healing journey and progress.

🔐 Account Security

MoveOn uses Firebase services for authentication and data storage. Keep your account credentials private and never share your password or authentication information with anyone.

🗑️ Reset Your Data

You can use the "Reset My Data" option from your Profile page to remove supported MoveOn progress data such as journal, moods, challenges, calm sessions and progress information.

🚫 What You Should Avoid Sharing

For your own safety, avoid entering passwords, bank details, card numbers, government ID numbers or other unnecessary sensitive information into MoveOn.

💜 MoveOn is a personal wellness and self-reflection tool. It is not a replacement for professional medical or mental health care.
