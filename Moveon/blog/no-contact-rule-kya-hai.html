No Contact Rule Kya Hai? Breakup Ke Baad Kaise Use Karein

No contact ka purpose manipulation nahi hai. Ye ek personal boundary ho sakti hai jisse breakup ke baad emotional space mil sake.

No contact ka simple meaning

Kuch time ke liye unnecessary calls, messages aur social-media checking ko stop ya limit karna. Practical exceptions situation ke according ho sakti hain, especially shared responsibilities me.

Kya no contact ex ko wapas laata hai?

Iski guarantee nahi hoti. No contact ko kisi ko control karne ki technique ke bajay apni healing aur boundaries ke liye use karna healthier approach hai.

Kitne din no contact?

Koi universal number nahi hai. Aapka goal emotional stability, routine aur clarity hona chahiye—not a fixed countdown.

No contact ke dauran kya karein?

Workout, sleep routine, study/work, friends/family, journaling aur hobbies ko intentionally schedule karein.

Agar message aa jaye?

Turant reply karna zaroori nahi. Pehle decide karein ki reply karna aapke goals aur boundaries ke according hai ya nahi.
