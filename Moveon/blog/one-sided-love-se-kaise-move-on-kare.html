One Sided Love Se Kaise Move On Kare? 8 Helpful Steps

One-sided feelings emotionally difficult ho sakti hain. Healing ka focus kisi ko convince karna nahi, apni self-respect aur emotional balance ko rebuild karna hai.

Reality ko accept karein

Agar feelings mutual nahi hain, repeated convincing usually situation ko healthy nahi banata. Clear reality ko accept karna difficult ho sakta hai, but it creates space for healing.

Hope aur evidence ko alag karein

Aap jo wish karte hain aur saamne wala actually kya communicate kar raha hai, dono ko separate dekhein.

Contact boundaries set karein

Agar frequent chats feelings ko stronger kar rahe hain, contact reduce karna ya temporary distance helpful ho sakta hai.

Self-worth ko outcome se separate karein

Kisi ka aapko choose na karna aapki complete worth ka measurement nahi hai.

New goals build karein

Fitness, career, study, hobbies aur friendships ko life ka active part banayein.

Final step

Aapko feelings ko instantly switch off nahi karna. Aapko bas un feelings ko apni poori life control karne se rokna hai.
