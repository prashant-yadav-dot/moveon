Ex Ko Kaise Bhule? Practical Ways To Stop Overthinking

Ex ko bhoolna usually memory delete karna nahi hota. Goal ye hota hai ki memories ke saath bhi aap apni life continue kar paayein.

Ex ki yaad baar-baar kyu aati hai?

Brain familiar routines aur emotional associations ko repeat karta hai. Isliye places, songs, photos aur specific times triggers ban sakte hain.

Trigger list banayein

3–5 common triggers note karein aur unke alternatives decide karein. Example: old playlist ki jagah new playlist, late-night profile checking ki jagah journaling.

Old chats aur photos ka kya karein?

Agar repeatedly dekhna painful hai to archive ya separate folder me move karna ek practical boundary ho sakta hai. Aapko har memory ko immediately delete karna compulsory nahi.

Overthinking ko interrupt karein

Thought aaye to uske saath argument karne ke bajay attention shift karein: 10 deep breaths, short walk, water, task list ya workout.

Apni attention ko rebuild karein

Ek meaningful daily goal choose karein. Focus gradually wapas aata hai; force karne se zyada consistency matter karti hai.

Ek important reminder

Agar emotional pain bahut intense ho ya daily functioning affect ho rahi ho, professional support lena appropriate ho sakta hai.

MoveOn provides general self-help information. It is not a substitute for professional mental-health care.
