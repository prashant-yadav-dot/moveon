Breakup Ke Baad Move On Kaise Kare? Step-by-Step Guide

Move on ka matlab memories erase karna nahi, balki apni present life ko dobara priority banana hai. Ye step-by-step guide isi process par focused hai.

Move on ka real meaning kya hai?

Move on ka matlab ye nahi ki aap kabhi ex ko yaad nahi karenge. Iska matlab hai ki memories aapki daily life aur decisions ko control na karein.

Step 1: Reality ko accept karein

Relationship khatam ho chuka hai to baar-baar imaginary scenarios banana pain ko prolong kar sakta hai. Facts aur hopes ko alag karna helpful hai.

Step 2: Digital distance banayein

Profile checking, old photos aur chats ko repeatedly revisit karna triggers ko active rakh sakta hai. Temporary mute/archive/block jaisi boundaries apni situation ke according use karein.

Step 3: Empty time ko healthy activities se fill karein

Workout, study, skill learning, family time aur hobbies ko schedule me rakhein. Busy rehna emotions ko suppress karna nahi, life ko structure dena hai.

Step 4: Memories ko process karein

Journal me likhein: relationship se maine kya seekha, meri boundaries kya hain, aur future relationship me mujhe kya chahiye.

Step 5: Rebound decisions se bachein

Sirf loneliness se bachne ke liye immediately new relationship start karna zaroori nahi. Pehle apni emotional needs samajhna useful ho sakta hai.

Step 6: Progress ko small wins me dekhein

Ek din profile check na karna, better sleep, workout complete karna ya focused study — ye sab recovery ke practical signs hain.

Final thought

Aapko past ko hate karne ki zarurat nahi. Bas apne present ko itni importance deni hai ki past aapki poori identity na ban jaye.
