How to Stop Missing Your Ex: A Practical Guide

Missing an ex does not automatically mean you should get back together. It can simply mean your brain is adjusting to a major change in routine and attachment.

Why do I still miss my ex?

Familiar routines, places, songs, messages, and emotional associations can trigger memories. Missing someone is not the same thing as needing to contact them.

Identify your biggest triggers

Write down when the memories hit hardest. Late nights, certain songs, social media, or specific places can each require a different boundary.

Create distance from repeated checking

If checking an ex’s profile makes you feel worse, mute or unfollow temporarily. The goal is to protect your attention.

Interrupt the thought loop

When you notice repetitive thinking, shift into a concrete activity: walk, shower, workout, breathing exercise, journaling, or a small work task.

Build new associations

Try new places, playlists, routines, hobbies, and goals. Your life can gradually accumulate experiences that are not connected to the relationship.

Do not romanticize only the good parts

When you miss someone, the mind may replay positive memories while ignoring the reasons the relationship ended. Try writing a balanced list of what worked and what did not.

Give yourself time

You do not have to stop caring overnight. The practical goal is to keep living while the intensity of the memories gradually changes.
