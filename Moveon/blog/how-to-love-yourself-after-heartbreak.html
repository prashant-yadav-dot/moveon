How to Love Yourself After Heartbreak: 9 Simple Practices

Self-love after heartbreak is less about feeling confident every day and more about treating yourself with patience while rebuilding your life.

1. Speak to yourself like a friend

Replace harsh self-judgment with factual and compassionate language. You can acknowledge mistakes without attacking your identity.

2. Keep promises to yourself

Start small: sleep on time, finish a workout, study for 30 minutes, or take a walk. Keeping small promises rebuilds trust in yourself.

3. Protect your boundaries

You are allowed to limit conversations, social-media exposure, or situations that repeatedly destabilize you.

4. Take care of your body

Regular movement, adequate sleep, balanced meals, and basic personal care can support a stable routine.

5. Journal without judging yourself

Write what happened, what you feel, what you learned, and what you want next. You do not need perfect answers.

6. Reconnect with your interests

Return to hobbies and goals that existed before the relationship or discover new ones.

7. Stop using someone else's attention as your score

Your value is not determined by whether one person chooses you.

8. Build a future that is yours

Set goals that do not depend on a relationship: education, career, fitness, creativity, travel, or financial independence.

9. Ask for support when needed

If distress is persistent or significantly affects daily life, a qualified professional can provide individualized support.

MoveOn provides general self-help information. It is not a substitute for professional mental-health care.
